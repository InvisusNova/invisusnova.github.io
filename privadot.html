Capabilities

Built with Total Privacy in Mind

Comprehensive security features engineered without battery drain

🔴

Real-time Privacy Dots

Displays a subtle, customizable indicator dot on top of your screen whenever an app activates your Camera (Green), Mic (Orange), or Location (Blue).

📜

Access History Log

Keep a chronological timeline of which app accessed what sensor, exact timestamp, and duration of access.

🎨

Full Dot Customization

Change dot colors, screen corner positions, opacity, size, and screen offset coordinates according to your preference.

🔒

Encrypted Local Database

All sensor logs are stored purely on your phone via encrypted SQLCipher SQLite database with hardware Keystore keys.

🔋

Zero Battery Impact

Operates using native Android AppOps & Camera callbacks. No heavy polling or battery draining background loops.

📱

Universal Device Support

Optimized for all Android brands including Xiaomi (MIUI/HyperOS), Vivo (Funtouch), Oppo/Realme (ColorOS), Samsung (OneUI), and Google Pixel.

Workflow

How PrivaDoT Works

Effortless 3-step setup to secure your device

1. Install & Grant Access

Enable Accessibility and Overlay permissions so PrivaDoT can observe hardware access events and draw indicators.

2. Silent Protection

PrivaDoT runs silently in the background with battery optimization exemptions without slowing down your phone.

3. Real-Time Alerts

Whenever an app secretly triggers your camera, mic, or location, an instant color-coded dot glows on your display.
