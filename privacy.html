Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 23, 2026

1. Introduction

At InvisusNova, your privacy is not an afterthought — it is the fundamental core of our architecture. This Privacy Policy details our strict commitment to zero personal data harvesting across our website and software applications (including PrivaDoT).

2. Applications (e.g. PrivaDoT)

  • No Telemetry: Our applications do not embed analytics trackers, crashlytics, or advertising networks.
  • No Cloud Transmissions: All sensor monitoring logs, settings, and database tables remain stored locally on your device in encrypted SQLite databases.
  • No Internet Permission: Our primary privacy monitoring tools intentionally omit network permissions, guaranteeing that data cannot physically leave your phone.

3. Web Tools Hub

All online utilities available under our /tools domain run purely via client-side JavaScript in your browser sandbox. We do not store, log, or transmit the passwords, strings, or images you process through our tools.

4. Website Visitor Information

This website is hosted via GitHub Pages. We do not deploy tracking cookies, session recording pixels, or invasive behavioral profiling.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, feel free to contact us at InvisusNova@proton.me.
